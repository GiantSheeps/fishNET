fishNET run assessment -- 2026-09-24
=====================================

Run assessed: runs/global_clim_5yr_gui_2
  - 180x80 global grid (2 deg), 23 species, looped GLORYS climatology forcing
  - config asks for days = 3650; the run stopped at day 3270 (~9 years)
  - fishing was off for every species (fishing_F = 0)
  - the run predates the generation-tracking and spin-up changes of 2026-09-23
Sources: the dashboard Validation tab (obsval.py: OBIS, RAM Legacy, ICCAT), and the run's
own series.nc (biomass, mortality by cause, stage structure, traits) and fields.nc.

Overall: the model is numerically solid and gets many species' latitudes right, but most
populations are not at a believable balance yet. Some start far from equilibrium, several
grow without limit, a few collapse, and a handful end up in the wrong ocean.


WHAT IS WORKING
---------------
- Stability: 9 years with no blow-ups; nitrogen budget error still ~4e-15.
- Plankton: P, Z and D settle within ~2 years into a repeating seasonal cycle, no drift.
- Latitude ranges: herring, capelin, pollock, cod, halibut, polar cod, silverfish,
  toothfish, Greenland halibut, skipjack and swordfish centre within ~5 deg of OBIS
  records. Across all species, range-centre latitude correlates at r ~ 0.87.
- Presence (AUC of model biomass vs OBIS record cells): ~0.9 for herring, capelin,
  polar cod and Atlantic bluefin; ~0.85 for halibut and silverfish. Atlantic bluefin
  also matches ICCAT catch by 5-degree square (Spearman rho = 0.67).
- Settled populations: hake flat at ~48 Mt since year 4; pollock and Pacific bluefin
  grow slowly and steadily.
- Assessed biomass: pollock within x1.5 of RAM Legacy; Atlantic bluefin x2.5,
  skipjack x2.7, European sardine x3.5 (some overshoot is expected with no fishing).


WHAT NEEDS WORK (most important first)
--------------------------------------
1. Starting state far from equilibrium.
   Total fish fell from 3.0 Gt to 1.0 Gt. Mesopelagics lost 99% (lanternfish
   1970 -> 27 Mt, bristlemouth 985 -> 2 Mt); others grew 10-400x (albacore
   0.8 -> 237 Mt, flounder 4.9 -> 321 Mt). A 30-day spin-up settles plankton and early
   losses; fish balances take years.
   Suggest: a "warm start" from a finished run's restart (fish state, clock reset), and
   starting biomass per species from assessments / the literature.

2. Nothing holds some species back.
   At year 9 several are still growing every year: swordfish +28%, skate +20%,
   halibut +16%, flounder +8%. Their biomass is 88-97% adults, and 72-97% of deaths are
   the fixed background ("natural") mortality. The only crowding penalty (K_nursery)
   applies to larvae and juveniles, so adults are never limited. Flounder takes 0.9 of
   its diet from detritus, which never runs out.
   Suggest: fishing (RAM Legacy F per species); adult crowding mortality or a
   food-limited ceiling; cap detritus feeding at the flux that reaches the sea bed.

3. Collapses with specific causes.
   - Yellowfin: extinct in year 1, never laid an egg. Started in temperate sectors and
     down to 250 m, far below its preferred 25 +/- 4 C (heat stress 34% of deaths);
     juveniles never reached maturity size (105 cm).
   - Toothfish: -81%, starvation 96% of deaths; its main prey, silverfish, fell 99.8%.
   - Greenland halibut: -92%, temperature stress 52%, starvation 29% of deaths.
   - Herring: grew to 57 Mt then fell to 0.05 Mt. Anchoveta and European sardine also
     boom and bust.
   Suggest: tropical-only start for yellowfin and check juvenile growth to maturity;
   broaden toothfish diet (lanternfish, bristlemouth); use the Populations tab's
   mortality-by-cause chart for each collapsing species.

4. Large pelagics rarely reach adulthood.
   Adults are only 1-5% of albacore, bluefin and swordfish biomass (yellowfin none);
   losses run 8-20x biomass per year (skipjack 19.8/yr). They mature at 90-190 cm and
   their juvenile crowding constant is small (K_nursery = 0.5), so juveniles in dense
   patches die several times faster. This is also why their generation counts are low.
   Suggest: raise K_nursery for oceanic species, or make the crowding penalty fade with
   size; check juveniles reach maturity size in 2-5 years.

5. Some species in the wrong place (range-centre latitude, model vs OBIS):
     European sardine   36 S vs 42 N
     flounder            3 S vs 59 N
     hake               28 N vs 47 N
     Pacific bluefin    40 S vs 17 N
     lanternfish        12 N vs 53 N
   Anchoveta also ends up outside the Humboldt Current. Starting regions are too wide
   (flounder is started in the southern temperate zone) and nothing keeps a stock in its
   region afterwards. Part of this is the validation: model "lanternfish" and "flounder"
   are generic groups, but were scored against one North Atlantic species each
   (Benthosema glaciale, plaice).
   Suggest: family-level OBIS for group species (Myctophidae, Pleuronectidae); restrict
   starting regions to real ranges; stronger homing for stocks.

6. No real low-oxygen zones.
   Lowest oxygen anywhere is 140 mmol m-3; real low-oxygen zones are below 20. The
   metabolic index is 5-58 for every species, so hypoxia never kills anything.
   Suggest: initialize oxygen (and nitrate) from World Ocean Atlas climatology and relax
   deep oxygen toward it instead of the constant 220.

7. Biomass levels against assessments: only 6 of 18 species within x10.
   Too high: hake x254, swordfish x138, flounder x66, albacore x63, cod x46.
   Near zero: herring, capelin, anchoveta, yellowfin, Greenland halibut.
   No fishing explains maybe x2-5, not x100; these should improve with the fixes above.


SUGGESTED ORDER
---------------
1. Parameter changes: tropical-only start for yellowfin, broader toothfish diet,
   narrower starting regions, family-level validation species.
2. Fishing from RAM Legacy fishing rates.
3. Adult crowding mortality and a cap on detritus feeding.
4. Warm start from an equilibrated restart, then a long spin-up run.
5. World Ocean Atlas oxygen.
Re-check the Validation tab after each step.
