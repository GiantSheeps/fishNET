fishNET run assessment -- 2026-09-26
=====================================

Run assessed: runs/global_clim_10yr_gui
  - 12 years (4380 days) on the 180x80 global grid, looped GLORYS climatology forcing
  - literature starting biomass, World Ocean Atlas 2023 oxygen, RAM Legacy fishing x 0.5,
    30-day spin-up (the changes of 2026-09-24)
Sources: series.nc, fields.nc, and the Validation tab / obsval.py (OBIS, RAM Legacy, ICCAT).


WHAT IS WORKING
---------------
- Stability: 12 years, no blow-ups; nitrogen budget error -5.7e-15; plankton flat after year 1.
- Low-oxygen zones hold: they shrank ~25% over the first 3 years, then stayed stable (~1000
  cells below 20 mmol m-3 at 450 m). Fish learned to avoid them: hypoxia deaths are now zero.
- Evolution shows up: maturity length falls in the fished species (cod -1.4 cm, skate
  -3.5 cm, Atlantic bluefin -1.0 cm, hake -0.7 cm), the direction of fishing-induced
  evolution. Temperature optima shift by up to 1.3 C; spawning dates by 1-4 days.
- Recovery from near-extinction: yellowfin fell to 0.002 Mt by year 2 and rebuilt to 250 Mt;
  Pacific bluefin also recovered.
- Latitudes: herring, pollock, capelin, halibut, polar cod and silverfish within ~5 deg of
  OBIS, AUC 0.73-0.91. Anchoveta improved (AUC 0.86 -> 0.93, rank correlation 0.22 -> 0.40).
- Assessed biomass close for herring (x0.99), pollock (x1.45), anchoveta (x2.7),
  yellowfin (x2.8).


WHAT IS UNREALISTIC, AND WHY
----------------------------
1. Upside-down food pyramid (the biggest problem). Tunas and swordfish total 1.4 Gt feeding
   on 18 Mt of lanternfish and bristlemouth. Against assessments: albacore x230, swordfish
   x810, Atlantic bluefin x21, skipjack x25. 85-99% of large-pelagic biomass is stunted
   "juveniles". Cause: every large pelagic has diet = { zoo = 0.15 } at every stage, so a 1 m
   tuna grazes copepods and a huge stunted population lives straight off zooplankton.

2. Species take over empty regions. European sardine peaked at 1.58 Gt; now 830 Mt, 84% in
   the Indian Ocean, none in the northern hemisphere. Atlantic bluefin is 51% in the Indian
   Ocean. No native forage fish or temperate tunas compete there, so the first arrival booms.
   Homing is almost inactive (1% of the time).

3. Boom-and-bust cycles far too large: European sardine swings three orders of magnitude on
   an 8-9 year cycle; herring 81 -> 8 -> 57 Mt. Real small pelagics vary ~10x over decades.

4. Fish mature too fast in gridded biomass. Albacore reached 19 generations in 12 years,
   skipjack 36, bristlemouth 62. Each step moves a fixed fraction of a class to the next
   stage whatever those fish's ages, so some mature almost immediately. Probably amplifies
   the booms, and inflates the generation counts.

5. Unchecked growth of long-lived bottom species: skate +38%/yr (0.17 -> 187 Mt), swordfish
   +11%, halibut +7%, cod +5%. Adults still face no crowding limit.

6. Mesopelagic collapse: lanternfish 1 Gt -> 334 Mt during the 30-day spin-up -> 15 Mt. The
   8 +/- 6 C temperature range is too narrow for a family spanning -2 to 28 C that rises to
   warm surface water at night. Losses 5.6x biomass per year; presence AUC 0.48 and 0.37.
   Toothfish starves (95% of deaths) because its prey collapsed.

7. Fishing unrealistic in structure: F constant in time and uniform over the whole ocean
   (including the Indian Ocean sardine). Inflated biomass inflates catches (47 Mt/yr of
   European sardine, 12 Mt of cod). Fishing causes at most 9% of any species' deaths.

8. F_ST reads exactly 1.000 for 5 species: too few agents per latitude band, not divergence.


SUGGESTED CHANGES (most important first)
----------------------------------------
1. Size-limited plankton feeding: apply the prey-size window to plankton too, so copepod
   feeding fades as fish grow and predators depend on forage fish.
2. More juvenile size classes in gridded biomass, so time to maturity has a realistic spread.
3. Adult crowding limits; cap detritus feeding at what reaches the sea bed.
4. Fill empty regions instead of walls: competitor groups (tropical Sardinella, other
   anchovies) and stronger natal homing.
5. Widen the mesopelagic temperature range; review their natural mortality.
6. Realistic fishing: year-by-year RAM F (1993-2021), only in the FAO areas of assessed
   stocks, at x1.0.
7. Real ocean forcing: GLORYS monthly from 1993 as a dated sequence.
8. Diagnostics: F_ST minimum agents per band; year-by-year RAM comparison in the Validation tab.

PATH TO THE 10-20 YEAR RUN
--------------------------
1. Implement 1-6 and the F_ST guard.
2. Short looped-climatology test: pyramid upright, invasions stopped?
3. Multi-year climatological spin-up at early-1990s fishing.
4. Warm start a 1993-2012 hindcast on real GLORYS with year-by-year RAM fishing.
5. Validate year by year against RAM, plus the maps.
